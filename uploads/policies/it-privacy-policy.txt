IT & DATA PRIVACY POLICY

This policy outlines the acceptable use of company technology and data protection requirements.

1. COMPUTER USAGE
- Use company computers for business purposes only
- Don't install unauthorized software
- Keep passwords secure and confidential
- Log out when leaving your workstation

2. INTERNET AND EMAIL
- Use internet for business-related activities
- Don't visit inappropriate websites
- Use professional language in emails
- Don't open suspicious emails or attachments

3. DATA PROTECTION
- Protect sensitive company information
- Don't share passwords or access credentials
- Use encryption for sensitive data
- Report data breaches immediately

4. MOBILE DEVICES
- Secure company mobile devices with passwords
- Don't install unauthorized apps
- Report lost or stolen devices immediately
- Use company devices for business only

5. SOCIAL MEDIA
- Don't post confidential company information
- Use professional language online
- Don't represent the company without authorization
- Follow company social media guidelines

6. BACKUP AND STORAGE
- Save work files to approved locations
- Don't store company data on personal devices
- Follow backup procedures
- Don't use unauthorized cloud storage

7. NETWORK SECURITY
- Don't connect unauthorized devices
- Use company VPN when working remotely
- Report network issues immediately
- Don't bypass security measures

8. PRIVACY COMPLIANCE
- Follow data protection regulations
- Handle personal data responsibly
- Report privacy concerns
- Complete privacy training

9. MONITORING
- Company may monitor computer usage
- Email and internet activity may be reviewed
- Acceptable use violations may result in discipline
- Report suspicious activity

10. VIOLATIONS
Violations may result in:
- Loss of computer privileges
- Disciplinary action
- Termination of employment
- Legal consequences

This policy applies to all employees and contractors.
