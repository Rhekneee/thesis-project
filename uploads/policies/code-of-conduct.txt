CODE OF CONDUCT

Our company is committed to maintaining the highest standards of ethical behavior and professional conduct.

1. ETHICAL STANDARDS
- Act with honesty and integrity
- Treat all individuals with respect and dignity
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Maintain confidentiality of company information

2. PROFESSIONAL BEHAVIOR
- Dress appropriately for the workplace
- Use appropriate language and communication
- Be punctual and reliable
- Take responsibility for your actions

3. WORKPLACE RELATIONSHIPS
- Foster a positive work environment
- Respect diversity and inclusion
- Avoid gossip and negative behavior
- Report inappropriate conduct

4. COMPANY RESOURCES
- Use company resources responsibly
- Protect company property and equipment
- Follow security protocols
- Report misuse of resources

5. CUSTOMER SERVICE
- Provide excellent customer service
- Maintain professional relationships
- Handle complaints appropriately
- Protect customer information

6. COMPLIANCE
- Follow all applicable laws and regulations
- Adhere to company policies and procedures
- Report violations or concerns
- Participate in required training

7. CONSEQUENCES
Violations of this code may result in:
- Verbal or written warnings
- Suspension
- Termination of employment
- Legal action if appropriate

8. REPORTING
Employees are encouraged to report violations to their supervisor or HR department.

This code applies to all employees, contractors, and visitors.
