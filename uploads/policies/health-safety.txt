HEALTH & SAFETY GUIDELINES

The safety and well-being of our employees is our top priority.

1. GENERAL SAFETY RULES
- Always wear appropriate safety equipment
- Report unsafe conditions immediately
- Follow all safety procedures and protocols
- Attend required safety training sessions

2. WORKPLACE HAZARDS
- Keep work areas clean and organized
- Store materials properly
- Use equipment according to instructions
- Report damaged or malfunctioning equipment

3. EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
- Know emergency exit routes
- Understand fire evacuation procedures
- Know location of first aid kits
- Report accidents and injuries immediately

4. PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE)
- Wear required PPE for your job
- Inspect PPE before use
- Replace damaged PPE immediately
- Store PPE properly when not in use

5. ERGONOMICS
- Maintain proper posture
- Take regular breaks
- Adjust workstations as needed
- Report discomfort or pain

6. CHEMICAL SAFETY
- Read safety data sheets
- Use chemicals according to instructions
- Store chemicals properly
- Report chemical spills immediately

7. ELECTRICAL SAFETY
- Don't overload electrical outlets
- Report electrical problems
- Don't use damaged cords
- Keep liquids away from electrical equipment

8. REPORTING REQUIREMENTS
- Report all accidents and injuries
- Report near-miss incidents
- Report unsafe conditions
- Participate in safety investigations

9. TRAINING
- Complete required safety training
- Attend refresher training
- Ask questions about safety procedures
- Share safety knowledge with others

10. COMPLIANCE
- Follow all safety regulations
- Adhere to company safety policies
- Participate in safety committees
- Suggest safety improvements

Remember: Safety is everyone's responsibility!
