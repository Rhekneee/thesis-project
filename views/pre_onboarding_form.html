Employee Handbook

Welcome to MDB Construction

This handbook serves as a guide to help you understand our company's policies, procedures, and benefits. It is designed to provide you with the information you need to be successful in your role.

Company Mission: To deliver exceptional construction services while maintaining the highest standards of quality, safety, and integrity.

Core Values:

  • Integrity and honesty in all business dealings
  • Commitment to quality and excellence
  • Respect for all team members and clients
  • Innovation and continuous improvement
  • Safety as our top priority

Employee Benefits:

  • Health insurance coverage
  • Retirement savings plan
  • Paid time off and holidays
  • Professional development opportunities
  • Performance-based bonuses
Code of Conduct

Ethical Standards

All employees are expected to maintain the highest ethical standards in their professional conduct. This includes:

  • Honesty and integrity in all business transactions
  • Respect for colleagues, clients, and vendors
  • Compliance with all applicable laws and regulations
  • Protection of company assets and confidential information
  • Fair and equal treatment of all individuals

Professional Behavior:

  • Maintain professional appearance and demeanor
  • Arrive on time and fulfill work commitments
  • Communicate respectfully and professionally
  • Report any unethical behavior or violations
Health & Safety Policy

Safety First

The safety and health of our employees is our top priority. All employees must:

  • Follow all safety protocols and procedures
  • Use appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE)
  • Report any safety hazards or incidents immediately
  • Participate in safety training programs
  • Maintain a clean and organized work environment

Emergency Procedures:

  • Know the location of emergency exits and equipment
  • Follow evacuation procedures in case of emergency
  • Report to designated assembly points
  • Cooperate with emergency responders
IT & Privacy Policy

Technology Usage Guidelines

Company technology resources are provided for business purposes. Employees must:

  • Use company equipment responsibly and professionally
  • Protect passwords and login credentials
  • Report any security incidents or breaches
  • Follow data backup and recovery procedures
  • Respect intellectual property rights

Privacy Protection:

  • Protect confidential client and company information
  • Follow data retention and disposal policies
  • Respect employee privacy rights
  • Comply with data protection regulations
Non-Disclosure Agreement

Confidentiality Requirements

As an employee, you will have access to confidential information. You agree to:

  • Keep all confidential information strictly confidential
  • Not disclose information to unauthorized persons
  • Use confidential information only for business purposes
  • Return all confidential materials upon termination
  • Report any unauthorized disclosure immediately

Confidential Information Includes:

  • Client lists and project details
  • Financial information and business strategies
  • Proprietary processes and technologies
  • Employee personal information
  • Any information marked as confidential
Terms & Conditions

Employment Terms

Your employment with MDB Construction is subject to the following terms:

  • Employment is at-will and may be terminated by either party
  • Compensation will be paid according to agreed terms
  • Work hours and schedule as determined by management
  • Compliance with all company policies and procedures
  • Performance expectations as outlined in job description

Termination Conditions:

  • Violation of company policies may result in termination
  • Poor performance may result in disciplinary action
  • Misconduct will result in immediate termination
  • Return of company property upon termination
